Case study

Sierra & Stone

Supporting a small business website with ongoing content maintenance, accessibility review, and a cleaner, easier-to-manage digital experience.

Project overview

Sierra & Stone needed a web presence that could be kept current without becoming confusing or difficult for staff to manage. The work focused on accurate updates, stronger structure, and easier day-to-day upkeep.

Challenge

As content changed over time, the website needed practical improvements to stay organized and useful for prospective customers who were looking for service details and business information.

Responsibilities

  • Website maintenance and content review
  • Quality checks for page clarity and consistency
  • Responsive updates and usability improvements
  • Accessibility review and instructional support
  • Ongoing support for site updates

Process and decisions

The site was improved through organized content review, simplified page structure, and routine testing across devices. The focus was on making content easier for site visitors to understand and easier for staff to maintain.

Website production details

Page updates, content cleanup, and QA review were used to keep the site current and easier to navigate. Small but consistent improvements reduced friction for both users and administrators.

Technical work or troubleshooting

Technical support focused on site behavior, form or page issues, and adjustments needed to keep the digital experience stable and accessible. Troubleshooting was targeted toward maintaining usability without overcomplicating the workflow.

Final solution

A clean, easier-to-manage web experience that supported better customer understanding and a healthier ongoing maintenance routine for a small business team.

Verified results or operational outcomes

Operational outcome: Easier content maintenance, stronger page clarity, and more consistent digital presentation for business needs.

Content note: No verified performance metrics were available for publication.

Lessons learned

Website maintenance is often a matter of reducing friction for both visitors and the people responsible for managing content. Clean structure and steady support make a website feel more reliable over time.
